The app opens on the Map with the first temple and room available. Open the first room, select cylinder 1, turn it forward once, and open the gate to complete the room. The Map, Museum, and Journal tabs provide access to progression, artifacts, and records. Completing all three rooms in a temple unlocks the next temple.

Progress is saved locally. The app uses an app group and WidgetKit to share a progress snapshot with its small and medium Home Screen widgets; a widget link opens the next room or the Museum after completion. No account, purchases, advertising, or network service are implemented. The app requests no camera, location, microphone, or photo library permissions. The privacy manifest declares no tracking or collected data types and declares file timestamp API access. The interface is in British English.
