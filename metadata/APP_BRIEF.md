# Scarab Vault

## What it is

Scarab Vault is an offline puzzle game about restoring inscriptions in five temples. It is for players who enjoy working out how connected stone cylinders move and collecting the artefacts revealed by each room.

## Launch and onboarding

1. On a cold launch, the app may briefly show “Opening the expedition…” while it loads saved progress.
2. A new player lands on the “Map” tab. There is no separate onboarding. The map introduces “THE KEEPER’S EXPEDITION”, “Scarab Vault” and “Some doors open only when you understand them.”
3. The map starts at “Seals 0/5” and “0 / 15 rooms”. Tap “Continue expedition” to enter the first room, or tap “The Sunken Gate” and then “Continue exploration” or “The first inscription”.
4. In “The mechanism”, match the stone cylinders to “THE CARVED INSCRIPTION”, then tap “Open the gate”. “Ask for a hint” and “Consult the expedition journal” provide help.

If saved progress cannot be read, the app instead shows “Your expedition is safe to retry” and “Your saved expedition could not be read. Retry, restore the previous save, or start a new expedition.” The available controls are “Retry loading”, “Restore previous save” and “Start a new expedition”. The last opens “Start again? Existing progress on this device will be replaced.” with “Start new expedition”.

## Screens

### Map

The “Map” tab shows the expedition title, seal and room counts, a progress bar, “THE RIVER PATH” and the five temples:

- “The Sunken Gate”
- “House of Reeds”
- “The Mirror Court”
- “Tomb of the Tides”
- “Vault of the Dawn”

“Continue expedition” opens the first unfinished room. After all five seals are earned, it becomes “Return to the vault” and opens the final room again.

Each available temple opens its chapter screen. Temple cards show “TEMPLE 1 • EXPLORE”, for example, and “0 of 3 rooms restored”; completed temples show “SEALED”. Locked temples show “UNDISCOVERED” and a requirement such as “Complete Temple 1 to enter”. The footer says “An offline expedition • saved on this device”.

### Temple

A temple screen is titled “Temple 1”, for example, and displays its chapter heading, story and “THE CHAMBERS”.

“Continue exploration” opens the first unfinished chamber in that temple; when all three are complete, it opens the first chamber again. The chamber controls are “The first inscription”, “The keeper’s passage” and “The reflecting chamber”. An available chamber opens its mechanism; a completed one says “Restored • revisit”. Chambers unlock in order.

The five chapter subtitles and stories are:

- “WHERE THE RIVER BEGINS” — “Beneath the river silt, a doorway still holds the warmth of dawn. Three inscriptions wait for a patient hand.”
- “EVERY STONE HAS A NEIGHBOUR” — “Roots wind through the walls like ropes. Behind the carvings, bronze axles bind one stone to the next.”
- “AS ABOVE, SO BELOW” — “A dry pool reflects nothing, yet the stones remember symmetry. What moves on one shore stirs the other.”
- “ONE RISES, ONE FALLS” — “Hidden counterweights once measured the river. Balance their opposing motion to reveal the keeper’s chamber.”
- “ALL PATHS MEET THE SUN” — “Five cylinders share one ancient spine. Let each movement settle in order, and the expedition’s final door will open.”

### The mechanism

“The mechanism” shows the temple and room, room title, seal count, move count, target inscription and numbered cylinders. Tap a cylinder to select it; the screen reports, for example, “Cylinder 1 selected”. Matching cylinders receive a checkmark.

“Turn back” and “Turn forward” turn the selected cylinder according to the room’s scheme. “Open the gate” checks the entire arrangement and, when it matches, opens the restored-room screen.

“Ask for a hint” reveals a thought about the scheme. Further taps advance “Hint 1/3” through “Hint 3/3”, adding the scheme explanation and then a suggested next move, such as “Next move: cylinder 1, turn forward.” The app may display “Reading the stones…” while calculating. If the inscription already matches, it says “The inscription is restored. Open the gate.”

“Reset” opens “Reset this room? Your collected artefacts will remain in the museum.” The destructive “Reset room” control returns that room’s cylinders, move count and hint level to their starting state.

“Consult the expedition journal” opens the journal in a sheet; “Done” closes it. The screen also says “Take your time. Every turn can be undone.”

### Room restored and Temple complete

A successful gate opens “Room restored”, or “Temple complete” when all three rooms in the temple have been restored. The screen displays “THE GATE OPENS” or “CHAPTER RESTORED”, the recovered artefact and its story, “Added to your museum”, and “MOVES”, “HINT LEVEL” and “SEALS”.

“Explore the next room” opens the next chamber. After completing a temple, “Enter the next temple” opens the next temple’s first room directly. After the final room, the screen says “The vault remembers you.” and “All five temples are restored. Revisit any room from the map, or explore your complete collection.”

### Museum

The “Museum” tab is headed “The museum” and shows the number “of 15 artefacts recovered”. Before the first discovery, it shows “Your first discovery awaits” and “Restore a room’s inscription to uncover its artefact. Each piece has a place and a story.” “Explore the first room” opens the first mechanism.

Artefacts are grouped by temple. Unfound entries say “Unrecovered” and, for example, “Room 1 • restore to reveal”; they cannot be opened. A recovered entry shows its name and “Room 1 • view story”; tapping it opens “Artefact story”, with the artefact’s story, temple, chamber, discovery date and move count.

The artefacts are “Dawn Scarab”, “River Compass”, “Lotus Seal”, “Reed Pendant”, “Bronze Axle”, “Keeper’s Tablet”, “Twin Falcons”, “Stillwater Lens”, “Mirror Diadem”, “Tide Vessel”, “Balance Feather”, “Moon Sundial”, “Golden Spine”, “Sun Cartouche” and “Heart of the Vault”.

### Journal

The “Journal” tab is headed “Expedition journal”, with “THE KEEPER’S FIELD NOTES” and “Observe. Understand. Remember.” Its segmented control switches between:

- “Symbols”: the six signs “Scarab”, “Sun”, “Eye”, “Lotus”, “Falcon” and “Wave”, with their meanings and an explanation of the inscription.
- “Schemes”: diagrams and explanations for visited temples. The schemes are “Single stones”, “Linked stones”, “Mirror stones”, “Counterweights” and “The great cascade”. The screen says “New schemes are recorded when you enter a temple.”
- “Records”: restored rooms, their discoveries, move counts, hint levels and dates.

### Expedition journal widget

The small and medium “Expedition journal” widget is described as “Your next chamber and recovered artefacts.” It shows the next temple, recovered artefact count, seal count and progress. Tapping it opens the next unfinished room; after completion it opens “Museum”.

Before the app has provided progress, the widget can show “Open the app to begin”, “Open the first temple to begin.” and “Your progress will appear here”.

## Features

- Restore 15 rooms across five temples by matching carved inscriptions.
- Select and turn stone cylinders under five different schemes.
- Earn five temple seals and recover 15 artefacts with stories.
- Ask for up to three levels of hints, including a suggested next move.
- Reset a room and revisit restored rooms.
- Consult “Symbols”, “Schemes” and “Records” in the “Expedition journal”.
- Track progress from the “Expedition journal” widget.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- “Open the gate” does not advance until every cylinder matches the inscription. If it does not match, the app says “The gate is still. Match each cylinder to the inscription above, then try again.” Turn the cylinders or use “Ask for a hint”.
- Later chambers and temples are disabled until the preceding rooms are restored. A locked temple says, for example, “Complete Temple 1 to enter”.
- “Unrecovered” museum entries are disabled. Restore their rooms to reveal and open their stories.
- “Records” initially says “The first page is yours to write” and “Restored rooms will appear here with their discoveries, moves and hints.” Restore a room to add a record.
- “Schemes” grows as temples are entered. It begins with the first temple’s scheme.
- “Reset room” resets the current attempt, including its moves and hints, while collected artefacts remain in the museum.
- After a temple is complete, “Continue exploration” returns to its first room. After the whole expedition is complete, “Return to the vault” revisits the final room.
- If saving fails, the app says “Your latest changes could not be saved. Keep the app open and retry saving.” Tap “Retry”. If recovery finds no readable previous save, it says “No readable previous save was found. Your existing files have been kept.”

## Starter content and resume

The five temples, 15 prepared puzzles, symbol guide, first scheme and artefact stories are built in. No rooms or artefacts start completed.

Unfinished cylinder arrangements, selected cylinder, move and hint counts, discoveries and progress are saved on the device. “Continue expedition” resumes the first unfinished room.

Expedition data stays on this device. There is no on-screen Contact or Support control.

None. The app does not scan barcodes or QR codes and does not show health, medical or product-health information.

## Platform

The interface uses English with British locale formatting; no region-specific content or region selection is shown. It supports iPhone and iPad, with a minimum of iOS 17.0. iPhone supports portrait and both landscape orientations; iPad also supports upside-down portrait.
